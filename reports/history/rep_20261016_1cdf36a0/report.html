Measurement Methodology & Reproducibility

ExecLens guarantees benchmark provenance through controlled isolation:

  • Phase Isolation: Build time, correctness validation, warm-up iterations, and steady-state runs are timed independently.
  • Kernel MaxRSS: Peak physical memory is queried via Linux kernel getrusage.
  • Statistical Rigor: Student's t 95% Confidence Intervals are calculated for all mean durations.
  • Truthful Hardware Diagnostics: Missing or architecture-incompatible accelerators are logged transparently without silent fallback.
